# Neon Hands Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Neon Hands is an offline Rock Paper Scissors game. The application does not collect, process, share, or sell personal data.

## Data

- No account is created.
- The application does not request names, email addresses, locations, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection SDK is used.
- The application requests no Internet permission and sends no data to a server.
- Only music and sound-effect volume preferences are stored locally in the application's private device storage. These preferences never leave the device and are removed when the application is uninstalled.

## Children's privacy

The application does not collect personal data from children or adults. A parent or guardian may use the contact channel below for privacy questions.

## Changes

If the application's data practices change, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before release.
