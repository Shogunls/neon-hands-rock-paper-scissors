## Full description

Gloves ready — the arena is waiting!

Neon Hands transforms the classic Rock Paper Scissors game into a colorful mobile duel with expressive 3D hands, smooth animation, and original sound. Choose your move, follow the rhythm, and watch both gloves reveal their final pose.

Highlights:

- Single-round and first-to-three modes
- Expressive 3D glove animation and physics-inspired movement
- Victory confetti and a cheerful fanfare
- Calm menu music and a competitive battle theme
- Separate music and sound-effect controls
- No ads, accounts, or in-app purchases
- Fully offline with no personal-data collection

Choose your move and become the arena champion!
